    Your rules

    • Rest days: about 3 L total fluids.
    • Training days: 3.5–4 L, and the 1 L Gatorade during the workout counts.
    • Drink to thirst and aim for pale-yellow urine.
    • Keep intake about the same each day, because swings in water move the scale.
    • Don't drink more than about 1 L per hour.
