Double progression

Keep the same weight until every set reaches the top of the rep range. Then add the smallest jump and build the reps back up.

Built on your program as it is in Strong (section 10 of your plan). Nothing in the exercise list changes. This page only tells you what to load and how many reps to chase next time.

The loop

  1. Pick the range8–12 for most lifts. 10–15 for small cable and dumbbell lifts, 12–20 for hip machines.
  2. Add repsSame weight. Beat last time by 1 rep on at least one set, stopping 1–2 reps short of failure.
  3. Hit the topWhen every working set reaches the top of the range with clean form, the lift is ready.
  4. Add loadNext session, add the smallest jump. Reps drop back near the bottom. Start again at step 2.

Jump sizes

EquipmentSmallest jumpYour lifts

Small lifts get a wider range because their jumps are huge in percentage terms. Going from 7.5 to 10 kg on cable laterals is a 33% jump, so you need 15 reps at 7.5 kg before 10 kg will hold 10 reps.

How to run it

Effort

Stop each working set 1–2 reps short of failure. On the last set of a small isolation lift you can go to failure. Warm-up sets never count.

Same reps, same rules

A rep only counts with the same depth, range and control as last time. Bouncing or cutting range to add a rep isn't progress.

Rest

2.5–3 min on bench, presses, rows, pulldowns and hack squat. 1–2 min on isolation lifts. Keep rest the same each session so reps compare fairly.

Logging in Strong

Use the Previous column as the target. Log every working set. Track Lower A and Lower B separately, because the lift order is different and the numbers will be too.

One 8-day cycle

Each workout comes around once per cycle, so a lift gets one chance to progress every 8 days. Expect about one rep per lift per cycle at best.

Treadmill after

Keep the 30 min walk after lifting, never before. At 4 km/h and incline 3 it won't hurt your next session.

While cutting

At 2,000 kcal, holding your current weights and reps is a win. Rep gains will be small and uneven, and some weeks nothing will move. Strength that holds while the scale drops means you're keeping muscle.

When a main lift drops

  • One bad session: ignore it and repeat the same targets.
  • Drops 2+ sessions in a row: check sleep, Meal 1 at 2 pm plus the Gatorade, and total volume before you touch calories.
  • No new reps for 3 sessions at the same weight: take 5–10% off, rebuild to the top of the range, then try the jump again.
  • Several main lifts stall together: deload for one cycle with the same weights, half the sets, and 3–4 reps left in the tank.

Plan a deload every 6–8 weeks even if nothing stalls. The first one would land around mid-December 2026.
