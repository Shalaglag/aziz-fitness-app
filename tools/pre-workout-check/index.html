The checks

Targets come from your plan: 2,000 kcal and 160–170 g protein a day, Adonis daily, Gatorade inside the 2,000 on training days. Pre-workout guides are general sports-nutrition ranges, not rules: roughly 40–110 g carbs and 20–40 g protein in the meal 1–2 hours before lifting, with fat kept low so it digests in time.

Items marked est. are estimates until you confirm them (Gatorade label, khubz loaf weight, eggs cooked in oil or butter).
