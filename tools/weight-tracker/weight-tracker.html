Reading the numbers

14-day strict window, Oct 25 to Nov 7. Keep calories near 2,000. The first 7-day average lands Oct 31; review calories only after Nov 7, from the average trend. Don't cut to 1,800 or 1,700 because of one weigh-in.

Week 1 drops fast. Water and glycogen leave first, so the early rate will look better than the real fat loss. The weekly rate switches from an early estimate to average-vs-average once there are two full weeks.

Saturday may read high after Friday's salty Cajun shrimp. Sodium, carbs, creatine, hard workouts and digestion all move the scale day to day.
