What each one does

Creatine monohydrate

5 g / dayMost useful on a cut
What it does
Helps your muscles refill quick energy between reps, so you keep strength and a rep or two more while eating 2,000 kcal.
Will you feel it?
No. The effect builds over weeks and shows up in the logbook, not as a buzz.
Side effects
Rarely a bloated stomach if taken as one big dose. At 5 g this is uncommon. No need to load or cycle it.
When
Any time. Pinned to Meal 1 so it becomes a habit. Mixing it into the Gatorade is fine.

It holds about 1–2 kg of water inside the muscle. That water is stable, so it doesn't distort your 7-day averages. Don't stop it because of the scale. If you get a blood test, tell the doctor you take it, since it can nudge creatinine up a little.

Vitamin D3

2 × 2,000 IU = 4,000 IU / dayAt the upper limit
What it does
Supports bones, muscle function and the immune system. Low vitamin D is common in Kuwait because most days are spent indoors.
Will you feel it?
Usually not, unless you were low to begin with. Then some people notice better energy over a few weeks.
Side effects
None expected at 4,000 IU. Problems only come from long-term doses well above this.
When
With a meal that has some fat, so Meal 2 in the evening. On Saturday the egg breakfast works too.

4,000 IU is the daily upper limit for adults. Don't go higher without a 25-OH vitamin D blood test.

Omega-3 (fish oil)

1 capsule · 180 mg EPAOptional upgrade: 2–3
What it does
Supports heart health and lowers blood triglycerides. It may help joints a little. Since you never eat fish, this is your only source.
Will you feel it?
No. It works quietly over months.
Side effects
Fishy burps. Taking it with a meal helps, and so does keeping the bottle in the fridge.
When
With Meal 2, together with the D3.

One capsule is a low dose. A common target is about 250–500 mg EPA + DHA a day. So 2–3 capsules is a sensible upgrade for someone who doesn't eat fish. Check the label for DHA per capsule to see where you land.

Magnesium bisglycinate

~100 mg elemental per capsuleEvening
What it does
Supports muscle and nerve function. You lose some in sweat from lifting and the treadmill. It may help sleep a little if you run low.
Will you feel it?
Some people feel slightly more relaxed at night. Many feel nothing.
Side effects
Large doses can loosen stools. The bisglycinate form is the gentlest on the stomach.
When
30–60 minutes before bed.

Keep total supplement magnesium at 350 mg a day or less (about 3 capsules of yours) unless a doctor says otherwise.

C4 pre-workout

1 scoop, training daysNot confirmed
What it does
Mostly caffeine, plus beta-alanine and citrulline. It improves focus and effort for a session. It doesn't build muscle by itself.
Will you feel it?
Yes. You'll feel more alert within 20–30 minutes, and beta-alanine causes a harmless tingle in the face and hands.
Side effects
Jitters, a faster heart rate, and worse sleep if taken late. Caffeine per scoop differs by C4 version, so read your tub's label.
When
About 3:00 pm Sun–Thu or 9:30 am Saturday. Never on Friday.

You haven't confirmed whether you still use it, so it's off the checklist by default. Turn it on in the Today box if you do.

Simple rules

  • Missed a dose? Take it when you remember that day. If the day is over, skip it and carry on. Don't double up on D3.
  • Supplements don't change your calories. None of these count toward the 2,000 kcal.
  • Don't judge them by the scale. Creatine water is stable, and the weekly average is what counts.
  • Consistency beats timing. Same times every day matter more than the perfect minute.
